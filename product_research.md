# Product Research Notes

## Apple MacBook Air

- **Source:** https://www.apple.com/macbook-air/specs/
- **Reference price observed:** $1,299 for the first 13-inch configuration.
- **Observed specifications:** Apple M5 chip; 16GB unified memory; 512GB SSD; 13.6-inch Liquid Retina display; up to 18 hours video streaming; 2.7 lb / 1.23 kg.
- **Use in catalog:** Portable general work, software development, and study.

## Microsoft Surface Laptop

- **Source:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/surface/devices/surface-laptop
- **Reference price observed:** $849.99 for the 13-inch model.
- **Observed specifications:** 8-core Snapdragon X Plus processor; 13-inch PixelSense LCD touchscreen at 1920 × 1280 and 60Hz; up to 23 hours video playback.
- **Use in catalog:** Portable study, daily work, and Windows-on-ARM productivity.

## Apple iPad Air

- **Source:** https://www.apple.com/ipad-air/specs/
- **Observed specifications:** Apple M4 chip; 11-inch or 13-inch form factors; Liquid Retina display; available in Blue, Purple, Starlight, and Space Gray finishes; supports Apple Pencil and keyboard accessories.
- **Price treatment:** The product specs page does not present a reference price in the browser extract, so the interface will direct users to Apple’s official purchase page rather than assert a current price.

## Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 Series

- **Source:** https://www.samsung.com/us/tablets/galaxy-tab-s10/
- **Reference price observed:** From $1,099.99 before trade-in.
- **Observed specifications:** 12GB RAM with 256GB or 512GB storage configurations; anti-reflective display treatment; positioned for portable productivity.
- **Content constraint:** The official page also presents customer-review content. This catalog will not use, summarize, or simulate any reviews or ratings.

## Citation note

Product prices and configurations vary by region and promotions. The interface should label values as reference prices and provide official product-page links for verification.
